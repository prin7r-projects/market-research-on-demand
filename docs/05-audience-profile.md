# 05 — Audience Profile

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

A B2B operator or capital allocator who has to defend a category-shaping decision in front of a peer audience (board, IC, partner meeting, exec staff) inside the next 60 days, with a budget that can absorb $500-$30k in research without a procurement cycle.

**Filters that make the ICP**:
- Decision velocity: must produce a written deliverable for a meeting on a known date.
- Source-skepticism: their audience asks "where did that number come from?" and they cannot say "ChatGPT."
- Willingness to pay above $499 for a single report: rules out hobbyists and pre-revenue pre-fundraise founders.
- Repeat trigger: the same decision class recurs every 4-12 weeks (new geo, new competitor, new pricing cycle, new PortCo).

## Persona 1 — Renee (primary)

- **Role**: Founder/CEO of a Series A-B B2B startup, $5-30M ARR, 30-80 FTE.
- **Background**: Ex-McKinsey or ex-PM. Knows what a good deck looks like and is offended by a bad one.
- **Triggers**: board meeting, fundraise prep, pricing rework, new market entry, response to a competitor's launch.
- **Goals**: defend a position internally; ship a thoughtful POV externally; not lose two weekends per quarter.
- **Frustrations**:
  - Internal team is too close to the data; output is biased.
  - Consulting firms are too slow and don't share working files.
  - Her last 5 ChatGPT citations included one URL that 404'd.
- **Channels**: Lenny's Newsletter, Stratechery, FirstRound Review, On Deck, Reforge alumni Slack.
- **Buying authority**: signs $5-50k research POs without finance review.

## Persona 2 — Marcus (secondary)

- **Role**: Senior Associate / Principal at a $200M-$2B AUM growth equity or PE shop, or a Director of Strategy at a F500 corp dev / corporate strategy team.
- **Background**: 4-8 years post-MBA or post-IB.
- **Triggers**: deal screening, sector deep-dive for a partner, quarterly portfolio review, post-merger market re-mapping.
- **Goals**: produce one defensible memo per week; build a sector library; be seen as the "expert" on the sector.
- **Frustrations**:
  - Pitchbook gives company tiles, not category synthesis.
  - GLG calls cost $1.5k each and don't produce a written artifact.
  - Internal research analysts are slammed with the partners' top-3 priorities.
- **Channels**: Capital Allocators podcast, Acquired, Bloomberg Terminal, Tegus / AlphaSense, internal Slack.
- **Buying authority**: signs $1-15k POs with managing director nod.

## Anti-personas (we will say no)

### Anti-1 — The unfunded curious founder
- "I have an idea, can you research the TAM?"
- Cannot afford $499 sustainably; will demand revisions; will not return.
- **Reason to refuse**: the unit economics break. We are not a low-end PaaS.

### Anti-2 — Academic researcher
- "I need a literature review for a paper."
- Wants 50+ sources, our format is 8-15 anchor sources.
- **Reason to refuse**: format mismatch + we do not do peer-reviewed citation styles (APA/MLA/Chicago compliance).

### Anti-3 — The compliance-driven enterprise legal review
- "Can your dossier be cited in an SEC filing?"
- **Reason to refuse**: we are editorial, not regulatory; legal liability boundaries are misaligned. Refer to a specialty firm.

### Anti-4 — The "make my deck look smart" buyer
- Wants the dossier laundered into their pre-existing slide narrative.
- **Reason to refuse**: we sell the argument, not the rationalization. We will turn down a brief whose conclusion is pre-specified.
