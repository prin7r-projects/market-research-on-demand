# 06 — Sales Channels

The buyers we want do not respond to cold outbound or paid display. They respond to (a) a trusted voice writing about *their* problem, (b) a peer in their Slack/group recommending a tool that saved a deliverable, (c) a sample artifact that does the convincing on its own. The channel mix below is built on those three mechanisms.

## Channel ranking (Wave 2 batch 1)

| Rank | Channel | Why it fits | First-90-day investment | Expected CAC |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Sample-as-content** (publish 4 anonymized real dossiers) | The artifact is the argument. A senior reader scrolls one and either gets it or doesn't. | 4 dossiers × 8h editorial = 32h | ~$0 marginal; embedded in fulfillment |
| 2 | **Newsletter sponsorships** (Lenny's, The Information's Strategy, Stratechery house ads, Not Boring) | Targeted to founder-strategist + investor; they already opted into "I will read a 1,500-word essay." | $5-15k/slot × 3 slots | $250-450 per first paid brief |
| 3 | **Operator-Slack reciprocity** (Reforge alumni, On Deck Founders, ProductMinded, RevGenius) | Peer recommendation closes deals. Our seed customers post their dossier in their channel; we don't post. | 2h/wk relationship work | Negative CAC if peer-shared |
| 4 | **Founder-led content** (one essay/week on `/journal`, syndicated to Substack + LinkedIn) | Builds the editorial-rigor signal that justifies the price. | 4h/essay × 12 essays = 48h | Compounding; CAC drops over time |
| 5 | **Targeted outbound to deal-team analysts** (Apollo + a single 2-line pitch + sample link) | High-intent niche. PE associates respond if the artifact looks like work product. | 50 contacts/wk × 12 wk = 600 | $300 per first paid brief |
| 6 | **Podcast appearances** (Lenny, Acquired, Capital Allocators) | One slot drives 50-200 brief submissions if we have a sample to point at. | Pitch + prep × 4 slots | Negligible direct, high brand |

## Channels we will NOT use (and why)

- **Google Search Ads** — the buyer is not searching "buy market research." They are subscribed to a newsletter or asking a peer.
- **LinkedIn cold InMail** — saturation collapsed CTR; our buyer reads zero of these.
- **G2 / Capterra** — wrong category placement; SaaS-listing is the wrong frame for a research desk.
- **Webinars / virtual events** — high effort, low conversion for a $500-2,500 product. Revisit at $20k+ ACV.
- **Affiliate referral spam** — will dilute the editorial signal.

## Channel-specific copy hooks

- **Lenny's Newsletter slot**: *"Stop renting consultants. Start citing."* — landing matches.
- **Founder-led essay launch**: *"Why every B2B deck has a 404 problem"* — opens with three real broken citations from VC decks; sells the editorial layer.
- **Operator Slack first-message**: *"I had to validate a pricing change before the next board meeting. Used OneWeekBrief. Here's the dossier they shipped in 31 hours: [link]"* — never said by us; given to seed customers as a template.

## Funnel KPIs (90-day target)

- Landing visit → `/sample` view: 35%
- `/sample` → `/brief` start: 12%
- `/brief` start → paid completion: 60%
- 90-day total paid briefs: 60-80, of which 8-12 convert to Monitor subscription.
